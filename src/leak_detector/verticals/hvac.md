# HVAC Vertical Pack

## Economics
- Average service ticket: **$550** (blended repair / service)
- Average replacement ticket: **$7,500**
- Contribution margin per job: **40%** (assumed)
- Typical residential shop: **120 inbound calls / month**
- Missed call rate (no 24/7 coverage): **~45%**
- After-hours share of calls: **~35%**

## Funnel
Traffic → Lead → Contact → Qualified → Booked → Dispatched → Job → Revenue → Repeat → Referral

## Common Leaks (menu)
1. Missed calls / slow response (highest $)
2. Booking friction on website / mobile
3. Weak estimate / quote follow-up
4. Unworked leads / no systematic follow-up
5. Weak local visibility / GMB
6. Poor AI-search visibility
7. Weak reputation / unanswered reviews
8. Weak service pages
9. Weak maintenance-plan conversion
10. No systematic referral system
